# Mini Fridge

<img width="910" height="847" alt="Screenshot 2026-09-10 185216" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cf444fc1-acca-4b3b-a4fa-7af0a9ca1c90" />

A small mini fridge, all you do is put the ice into bottom compartment and place your cans above.
supports two cans, *MAX: 14.5cm x 17cm*

## Assembly
- The window on the door should be printed in a transparent/semi-transparent filament and then pushes into place on the door.
<img width="301" height="285" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d29b9af7-a76d-4ef2-8597-24a352c02b83" />

- Align the two hinge pieces on the door in-between the ones on the frame then push the nail through it to hold it in place.
<img width="496" height="698" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/29d3e8fd-6438-46a2-9e5f-71837e0823ed" />

- The ice drawer just slides into place.
